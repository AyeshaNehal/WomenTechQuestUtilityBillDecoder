### LESCO Glossary
- **FPA / FCA**: Fuel Price Adjustment / Fuel Charge Adjustment. Classify as `fpa`.
- **QTA / UQA**: Uniform Quarterly Adjustment. Classify as `quarterly_adjustment`.
- **GST**: General Sales Tax. Classify as `gst`.
- **ED**: Electricity Duty. Classify as `electricity_duty`.
- **F.C Surcharge**: Financing Cost Surcharge. Classify as `surcharge`.
- **Meter Rent**: Classify as `meter_rent`.
